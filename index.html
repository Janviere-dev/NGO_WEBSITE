Mission

Our mission is to support and empower rural youth, through sexual reproductive health, capacity building, and girls' education to improve their health and socioeconomic well-being.

Vision

We envision a society where rural youth can achieve their full potential in health, education, and socioeconomic well-being.

What we do

Sexual and Reproductive Health & Wellbeing

The Sexual and Reproductive Health & WellbeingProgram works to provide accurate, age-appropriate, and rights-based information on sexual and reproductive health to adolescents and women, with the aim of breaking deeply rooted myths, stigma, and misinformation and availing resources.

Read More

Girls’ Education & Life Pathways

The Girls’ Education & Scholarship Program works to ensure that girls from marginalized communities can access, remain in, and complete their education, while building their agency.

Read More

Women's Skills and Livelihoods Program (WSL)

The Women’s Social Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment Program supports young women to build sustainable livelihoods, become active members of the workforce, and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

People with Disabilities

We address the urgent need for sexual and reproductive health (SRH) education and support for girls in rural areas of Rwanda. We break the silence and provide comprehensive SRH education, focusing on empowering young girls and preventing challenges caused by lack of information.
